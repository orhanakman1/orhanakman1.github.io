Why Accounting Knowledge Matters in ERP

ERP & Accounting · October 2026

When people hear "ERP", they usually think about software. But an ERP system is first of all a mirror of the company's business. Almost every action in the system has a financial result. This is why I decided to get an accounting certificate.

Every transaction tells a financial story

When a sales order is created, an invoice is issued, or goods leave the warehouse, the system creates accounting records in the background. If you understand basic accounting concepts such as debit, credit, accounts receivable and accounts payable, you can understand why the system behaves the way it does.

How it helps me at work

  • Better communication: I can speak the same language as the finance team.
  • Finding errors faster: When a report looks wrong, I know where to look.
  • Seeing the big picture: I understand how a small change in one module can affect the financial reports.
Technology tells you how a process works. Accounting tells you why it matters.

Advice for people who want to work with ERP

If you are interested in ERP, do not focus only on the technical side. Learn the basics of accounting and business processes. It will make you a much stronger specialist and help you build better connections between departments.
