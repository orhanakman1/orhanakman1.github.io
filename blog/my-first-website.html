Lessons from Designing My First Website

Web Design · October 2026

My first real work experience was designing the website of Foursestudio, one of the most fun workshop teams in Konya. After that, I also worked on the web design of Dost Makine. These projects taught me lessons that I still use today.

1. Understand the client before you design

A workshop team and an industrial company need very different websites. Before choosing colours or fonts, I learned to ask: Who are the visitors? What do they want to do on the site? What feeling should the brand give?

2. Simple is better

At the beginning, I wanted to add many effects and sections. Later, I understood that visitors want to find information quickly. A clean layout and clear navigation are more valuable than decoration.

3. Mobile comes first

Most people visit websites from their phones. Testing every page on a small screen became one of my most important habits.

4. Feedback makes the work better

Every round of feedback from the client improved the result. I learned not to take criticism personally, but to see it as a tool for building a better product.

Design is not only how a website looks. It is how easily people can use it.

These lessons are not only useful for web design. Today, while working with ERP systems, I still try to think like a designer and put the user first.
