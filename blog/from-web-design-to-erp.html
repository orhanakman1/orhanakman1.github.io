From Web Design to ERP: My First Steps as an Assistant ERP Specialist

Career · October 2026

My career started with web design. At Foursestudio and Dost Makine, I learned how to listen to a client, understand their needs and turn them into a working product. Today, I work as an Assistant ERP Specialist at Akko, and I see that these two worlds are closer than they look.

What is an ERP system?

ERP stands for Enterprise Resource Planning. It is a system that brings the main processes of a company together in one place: purchasing, sales, stock, production, finance and human resources. Instead of every department keeping its own spreadsheets, everyone works on the same data.

What does an Assistant ERP Specialist do?

My daily work includes tasks such as:

  • helping colleagues when they have problems or questions about the system,
  • checking data and making sure records are correct and consistent,
  • supporting the ERP team when processes are defined or improved,
  • learning how each department works and how the system supports it.

What web design taught me for ERP

In web design, the most important question is: "Is this easy for the user?" The same question is valid for ERP. A system can be very powerful, but if people find it difficult, they will not use it correctly. My background in user experience helps me look at the system from the user's point of view.

Good technology is not only about features. It is about people being able to do their work faster and with fewer mistakes.

What's next?

I want to deepen my knowledge of ERP processes, especially on the finance side, and combine it with my technical skills. In the next posts, I will share more about what I learn on this journey.
